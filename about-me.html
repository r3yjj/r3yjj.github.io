About me

Hi, my name is Ray. I’m currently a student at both Ngee Ann Polytechnic (NP) and the Institute of Technical Education (ITE) in Singapore.


My dream has always been to travel around the world and explore different cultures and lifestyles. Growing up in Singapore, a small island nation, I feel that my experiences have been somewhat limited, and I believe there’s so much more out there waiting to be discovered. I hope to make this dream come true one day when I’m financially stable.


I also aspire to start my own skincare and cosmetics business after completing my studies. I’ve always been passionate about skincare & cosmetics industry, and I plan to focus on building this dream when I have the time and capital to do so.


For my personality wise, I’m friendly, easy-going, and open-minded. I enjoy making new friends and connecting with like-minded people. Since I'm naturally a curious person, it makes me enjoy fostering conversations with people from all walks of life and diverse backgrounds. For me, it’s not just about meeting new friends, but about understanding different perspectives and lifestyles.


During my free time, I love listening to and singing songs in various languages like V-pop, T-pop, K-pop, C-pop, and English pop, as music helps me feel refreshed and rejuvenated. I also value sleep as an important part of my self-care routine, that's why sometimes I prefer to sleep and rest if I have nothing to do. As an animal lover, I hope to have a rabbit as a pet one day, so I can perhaps spend time with them too when I'm free. 🐰
